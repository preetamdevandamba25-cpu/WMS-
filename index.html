    About the business

    Small and mid-sized businesses lose sales when stock is miscounted, orders ship late, or nobody knows which bin holds what. We fix that with disciplined warehouse processes and a system that records every movement.

    What we do

    We receive, store, pick, pack and dispatch goods on behalf of our clients, with bin-level inventory control and barcode-confirmed movements.

    Target customers

    D2C and e-commerce brands, FMCG and electronics distributors, pharma and auto-parts traders, and manufacturers who need overflow storage.

    Service area

    Primary hub in Vadodara, Gujarat, with same-day linehaul to Ahmedabad, Surat and Mumbai.

    Key value proposition

    • Accurate stock: cycle counts and scan-based putaway keep record and reality within 1%.
    • Faster orders: wave picking and slotting by velocity cut walking time.
    • Visibility: clients see inventory and order status in real time.
    • Lower cost: pay per pallet and per order, with no fixed building or labour cost.

    How an order moves through the warehouse

    1. InboundDock scheduling, ASN check, goods receipt
    2. PutawaySystem-directed bin assignment
    3. PickWave and zone picking with scanning
    4. PackWeigh, label, quality check
    5. DispatchCarrier handover and tracking ID
